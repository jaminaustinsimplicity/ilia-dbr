DBR Workflow — 13 Stages

End-to-end Death Benefit Reduction process · Policy Years 11–15 · 4 Signing Ceremonies

SIGNING CEREMONY #1
Client / Insured
DBR Election Form
Client selects YES or NO for death benefit reduction on the election form top section.
SIGNING CEREMONY #2
Settlor
Hold Harmless Section
Settlor (or entity authorized rep) signs the Hold Harmless & Indemnification section — always required regardless of YES/NO.
SIGNING CEREMONY #3
Trustee (NIW)
Carrier Policy Change Form
NIW Trust Services signs the Owner/Trust authorization section to approve and authorize the policy modification.
SIGNING CEREMONY #4
Bank Authorized Signer
Carrier Policy Change Form
Bank signer authorizes. One notary cert can cover multiple DBR transactions in batch. NOT sent the Election Form.

Signing Ceremonies — Workflow Details

4 signing ceremonies across the DBR process · Each with distinct documents, signers, and routing logic

✍ Ceremony #1 — Client / Insured Election

Stage 5
DOCUMENT
NIW – DB Reduction Final Revision
Section: Top election section
SIGNER
Client / Insured
Platform: ILIA (not DocuSign)
ACTION
Selects YES or NO checkbox
ILIA captures decision + timestamp

✍ Ceremony #2 — Settlor Hold Harmless

Stage 7
DOCUMENT
Hold Harmless & Indemnification
As to Provident Trust Group, LLC
SIGNER — ILIA ROUTING LOGIC
Scenario A — Insured IS Settlor:
Client signs both election + hold harmless

Scenario B — Insured NOT Settlor:
Client signs election → routes to Settlor

Entity Settlor: ILIA uses stored Authorized Signer Name + Email
RULES
Always required — regardless of YES or NO client decision.

Settlor can be Individual or Entity.

Both ceremonies can be combined if Insured = Settlor.

✍ Ceremony #3 — Trustee Authorization

Stage 9
DOCUMENT
Carrier Policy Change Form
Section: Owner/Trust authorization
SIGNER
Trustee — NIW Trust Services
Approves DBR + authorizes policy modification
ILIA POPULATES
Policy # · Insured Name
Trust Name · Reduction Amount
Revised Death Benefit

✍ Ceremony #4 — Bank Authorization

Stage 11
DOCUMENT
Carrier Policy Change Form ONLY
⚠ NOT the DBR Election Form
SIGNER
Bank Authorized Signer
ILIA stores: Bank Name, Signer Name, Signer Email
NOTARIZATION
After signing, bank notarizes documents in batch.

One notary cert can cover multiple DBR transactions — batch processing supported.
